# Course Catalog

The complete listing of every course in Roura-io Academy, numbered, weighted, and
prerequisite-chained. The [Program of Study](./PROGRAM-OF-STUDY.md) says *when* to take
things; this says *what everything is*.

**Last reviewed: 2026-08-13.**

## How to read an entry

**Codes.** `PREFIX NNN` — the first digit is level: `1xx` introductory (no prerequisites
beyond literacy), `2xx` intermediate, `3xx` advanced, `4xx` capstone or licensure.

**Credits.** Credit-equivalents on the Carnegie convention: **1 credit ≈ 45 hours** of
total student work. These are estimates for comparison and workload planning, not
accredited credit. See [Academic standards](./PROGRAM-OF-STUDY.md#academic-standards).

**Cost.** Unmarked entries are free to audit, read, or run. **(paid)** marks an
unavoidable cost — an exam fee, a statutory course, a vendor's instructor-led class.
**(free tier)** marks a service whose useful portion is free but which upsells.

A conventional bachelor's degree is 120 credits (~5,400 hours). The academy's four programs
together exceed 250 credit-equivalents, which is why the
[Program of Study](./PROGRAM-OF-STUDY.md) offers a sequential route — nobody should attempt
all of it in four years, and the plan says so.

## Notes on selection

**Where two courses cover the same ground**, both are listed and the Program of Study picks
one. CS 110–122 versus CS 130, MATH 220 versus MATH 221, NET 210 versus NET 211 — these are
alternates, not duplicates to be taken twice.

**Some entries are references, not courses** (marked with a `—` credit value): standards
bodies, documentation sets, and video libraries you return to rather than complete. They
carry codes so the Program of Study can point at them.

**Vendor training is labeled as such.** Cisco Networking Academy is free vendor training.
Ubiquiti Academy is paid, instructor-led vendor training. Neither is open courseware, and
neither is presented here as though it were.
